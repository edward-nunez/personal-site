Understanding our system design and the decisions that shaped it. This section documents both the current architecture and the reasoning behind major decisions.

## Why ADRs Matter

ADRs capture the "why" behind our architectural choices, not just the "what." When you encounter `packages/backend/src/domain/...` or understand why we use Drizzle with Clean Architecture, you're seeing past decisions documented in our ADRs. These prevent repeating mistakes and help decide when to refactor.

## When to Use This Section

- Trying to understand why the system is structured this way?
- Designing a new feature and want to follow established patterns?
- Proposing a significant technical change?
- Onboarding and need context on architectural principles?
